About Tappin

The Norwegian event technology company behind 500+ events a year, since 2012.

Tappin AS is the Norwegian event technology company building Your Event Solution from Oslo since 2012. Product folks, producers and problem-solvers, backstage at hundreds of Norway's biggest events. Tappin exists because we've felt the chaos ourselves, and decided to fix it.

Our story

From three founders in 2012 to Norway's trusted event platform.

What started as an idea between Ingar Hagen, Tor Østensen and Per Kaarby has grown into the platform behind 500+ events a year, without losing the practitioner's perspective.

November 2012

Tappin AS is founded in Oslo

Ingar Hagen, Tor Østensen and Per Kaarby set out with a simple idea: events deserve technology that respects both organizers and attendees.

September 2013

First mobile event app shipped

Delivered to Virkes Handelskonferansen, the first of hundreds of events we'd help run across the next decade.

May 2020

Ingunn Dahl-Engh joins as CEO

With 18+ years in technology leadership, Ingunn takes the helm at one of the most important pivots in the company's history.

March 2020

COVID pivot, fully digital in 8 weeks

When the world locked down, we rebuilt for fully digital events in under two months. The pivot turned 2020 into our best year on record.

October 2021

Acquisition of Mingl AS

Expanded our networking and attendee-matching capabilities, deepening the engagement layer of the platform.

August 2025

TGC Capital Partners investment

Strategic growth investment to scale Your Event Solution beyond Norway while staying true to our practitioner DNA.

2026, Today

500+ events. 200K+ participants. One calm flow.

Used across Norway's enterprises, public sector, and associations. Still made by people who've been backstage.

Industries we serve

The Norwegian event technology company built for every kind of audience

Tappin AS is the Norwegian event technology company powering events for finance, retail, energy, healthcare, public sector, recruitment and professional associations across Norway. The platform is sector-neutral, the support team isn't.

Finance & insurance

Gjensidige runs annual employee conferences and partner events on Tappin. The platform handles enterprise procurement workflows, DPA-standard data handling, and the security expectations of regulated industries.

Retail & consumer brands

Elkjøp Nordic runs HR and supplier events with Tappin across Norway and the Nordics. From recruitment fairs to leadership offsites, the platform handles multi-market complexity without multi-vendor chaos.

Professional associations

Tekna, NOKU, Sponsor- og Eventforeningen, Friluftsrådenes Landsforbund and EHiN run hundreds of member events on Tappin a year. Persistent member profiles, multi-event continuity, group bookings, built for the way associations actually work.

Technology & SaaS

TietoEVRY relies on Tappin for talent and customer events. Norwegian tech companies appreciate the platform's API access, native HubSpot and Salesforce integrations, and the fact that we're builders, not blockers.

Public sector & infrastructure

BaneNOR and Norwegian ministries trust Tappin with citizen-facing events, internal town halls and policy briefings. Norway-hosted data, EHF e-invoicing, and the compliance posture the public sector requires.

Energy, healthcare & beyond

Circle K runs station-manager events and partner days on Tappin. Healthcare, energy and industrial customers run training, supplier and customer events with the same calm flow.

Why Norway

Why Tappin stays a Norwegian event technology company

We could have moved the headquarters. We've chosen not to. The Norwegian event market is the most discerning we've ever served, and staying close to it makes the product better.

Norwegian event leaders are direct. They tell us when something doesn't work. They expect data on the table, not slides over coffee. They prefer a vendor that picks up the phone over one with a glossier landing page. The product is sharper because of it.

From Oslo, we serve customers across the Nordics, Norway first, with active customer events in Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Iceland. The platform is multilingual by design and our roadmap is intentionally regional, not "we'll get to that quarter when we have time."

The Tappin team includes ex-event-producers, ex-organizers and ex-attendees. We've been the one with the broken printer at 8:55am. The product reflects that.
