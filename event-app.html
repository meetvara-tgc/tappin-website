FAQ

Branded event app Norway, common questions

Is the branded event app Norway customers see actually fully branded?

Yes. Custom colors, logo, fonts, splash screen, hero image, custom domain (app.yourcompany.com), and event-specific themes. Attendees don't see "Tappin" anywhere unless you choose to credit us. The app feels like your brand built it in-house.

Do attendees need to download a mobile event app Norway from the App Store?

No. The Tappin event app runs as a Progressive Web App on any modern browser. Attendees click one link from email and the app installs to their home screen. For enterprise customers who want native iOS/Android, we offer the Tappin shell with your branding, no separate app-store submission needed per event.

What features are inside the attendee app?

Personalized agenda, push notifications, speaker bios with LinkedIn, sponsor profiles with lead capture, attendee directory and networking, AI-powered 1:1 meeting booker, digital business cards, interactive venue maps, live polls and Q&A, gamified missions, and personalized feedback prompts after each session.

Can we run a multi-day or multi-event experience?

Yes. The app supports multi-day agendas, multi-track events, recurring annual conferences, and persistent attendee profiles that span events. Member organizations particularly value the year-on-year continuity for delegates and sponsors.

How does the event app handle hybrid and digital audiences?

The same attendee app serves physical, hybrid and digital event audiences. Online attendees watch streams, network 1:1, and join Q&A from the same app in-room attendees use for personalized schedules and check-in QRs.

How long does it take to launch a branded event app?

Typically 5-10 working days from kickoff to a fully branded preview link you can share with stakeholders. Norwegian onboarding team builds the first event with you, including registration, check-in and app, in a single working session.
