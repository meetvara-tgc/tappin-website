FAQ

Answers to the questions event leads always ask first.

How long does it take to launch our first event on Tappin?

Most customers run their first event within 7-14 days. Onboarding is included, and our Norwegian team builds your first registration page with you in a single working session.

Is the platform GDPR-compliant and Norwegian-hosted?

Yes. Tappin is built GDPR-first, data is hosted in Norway, and we sign Data Processing Agreements with every customer. Enterprise-grade security and full audit trails are standard.

Can Tappin handle hybrid events with thousands of attendees?

Absolutely. Our largest customers run 5,000+ person hybrid events with simultaneous physical check-in, live streaming, networking, and Q&A. We scale to your event, not the other way around.

How do you integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, or our existing CRM?

Native integrations are available for HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics, Power BI, and Slack. For anything else, our open API and webhooks handle custom flows.

What does pricing actually look like?

Pricing is based on how many events and participants you expect, never per seat. Most customers spend less than they did across the 4-5 tools Tappin replaces. Book a demo for an exact quote within 24 hours.

Do you support events in English and other languages?

Yes. Tappin is fully multilingual, registration pages, attendee app, and admin can be set per event. Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German, and Spanish are supported out of the box.
