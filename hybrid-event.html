FAQ

Hybrid event platform Norway, common questions

What makes a hybrid event platform Norway different from a webinar tool?

A webinar tool streams a presentation. A hybrid event platform unifies registration, ticketing, check-in, streaming, networking, Q&A, polls, sponsor visibility and analytics, for both your physical room and your digital audience simultaneously. Tappin is the latter.

Does Tappin handle hybrid event streaming Norway with low latency?

Yes. Multi-room HD streaming is built-in, with backstage controls, recording, replay, and live captions. Stream latency is typically under 5 seconds so online Q&A keeps pace with the physical room.

Can in-room and online attendees vote in the same poll?

Yes, and this is the headline feature. Polls, Q&A with upvoting, and reactions are unified across both audiences. The speaker sees one combined result on the screen, regardless of whether votes came from the hall in Oslo or laptops in Tromsø. See the full feature set on the event engagement platform Norway page.

How does the hybrid event app work for online attendees?

The same branded event app serves both audiences. Online attendees use it to watch sessions, network 1:1 with people in the room, and participate in live polls. In-room attendees use it for personalized schedules, check-in QR, and post-session ratings. One app, two audiences, zero compromise.

What's the biggest hybrid event Tappin has powered in Norway?

Our largest hybrid event customers regularly run 3,000-5,000 person events with simultaneous physical check-in across multiple cities and a live stream for thousands more. The platform scales horizontally, there is no practical attendee ceiling for a Tappin hybrid event.

If we want to go fully digital later, do we need a new platform?

No. The same Tappin platform handles physical events, hybrid events, and fully digital events. Switch the format mid-cycle without re-platforming, re-buying or re-training your team.
