Editorial promise

Why this event management blog Norway exists at all

We're an event platform company, and we know how that usually shows up in a "blog." Our editorial promise to event leaders in Norway is simple, and we'd rather be measured against it than against any keyword ranking.

Every story published in Tappin Stories is written or reviewed by someone who has actually run, produced or operated an event in Norway. Not a content team, not an SEO agency, not a contractor. If a piece is theoretical, we say so. If it is opinion, we say so. If it comes from a customer's experience, we credit them by name.

We avoid generic "5 tips for event planning" content. Every story we publish is supposed to be useful to one specific reader, a head of events at a Norwegian organization who is currently planning something, currently negotiating with a vendor, or currently trying to justify event spend to leadership. If a draft fails that test, we don't ship it.

If you have a question we haven't answered, write to post@tappin.no. The good questions become the next stories.
