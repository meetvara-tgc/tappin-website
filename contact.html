Demo FAQ

Common questions before you book a Tappin demo

The questions Norwegian event organizers ask most before booking their first event platform demo. Don't see yours? Email post@tappin.no, we reply within two hours.

How long does a Tappin event platform demo take?

Twenty minutes is the standard. We respect your calendar, most of our customers are heads of events, HR or marketing leaders with 30-minute meeting blocks. If you have specific deep-dive needs (integrations, security, group registration flows), we'll book a follow-up technical session.

Do I need to prepare anything for the demo?

Nothing mandatory. The demo is more useful if you can share a rough event brief, expected attendees, format, dates, any pain points with your current event software. We tailor every demo to a specific event in your pipeline, so the more we know in advance, the more relevant the walkthrough.

Will I be talking to a salesperson or an event expert?

An event expert. Everyone on the Tappin team running demos has produced or managed events themselves. You will not be put through a discovery, qualification and demo gauntlet. One call, one person, one clear outcome.

Can I bring procurement, IT or legal to the demo?

Yes, please do. Norwegian enterprise procurement teams often want the GDPR, DPA and hosting story upfront. We're happy to cover compliance, integrations and pricing structure in the same call rather than splitting it across three meetings.

Is there a charge for the event platform demo in Norway?

No. The demo is free, the follow-up pricing is free, and there is no obligation. Many event leaders use the demo to benchmark Tappin against their current stack, that's perfectly fine.

What's your typical pricing for the Tappin event platform?

Pricing is scoped per organization, based on how many events and participants you expect per year, not per seat. Most Norwegian customers spend less on Tappin than the 4-5 tools we replace. You'll have a tailored quote in writing within 24 hours of the demo.
