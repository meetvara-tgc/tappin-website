Event Platform DPA Norway, Tappin Data Processing Agreement

The event platform DPA Norway customers sign with Tappin AS. This Data Processing Agreement defines the terms under which we process personal data on behalf of our customers, included in every contract, GDPR Article 28 compliant.

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the master services agreement between Tappin AS ("Processor") and the customer ("Controller") who uses the Tappin platform. It governs the processing of personal data carried out by Tappin on the Controller's behalf.

1. Purpose

The purpose of this DPA is to ensure that personal data processed by Tappin on behalf of the Controller is handled in accordance with the GDPR and applicable Norwegian law. This DPA constitutes the data processing agreement required by GDPR Article 28.

2. Scope of processing

Tappin processes the following categories of personal data on behalf of the Controller, depending on the configuration of the customer's event(s):

  • Identification data, name, email, phone, organization
  • Professional data, title, role, industry
  • Event participation data, registrations, check-ins, sessions attended, engagement actions
  • Networking data, meeting requests, messages, profile data
  • Payment data, handled by certified PCI-DSS payment subprocessors

Personal data is processed for the duration of the customer agreement, plus a 30-day grace period for data export, after which it is permanently deleted.

3. Processing instructions

Tappin processes personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of personal data to a third country or international organization, unless required to do so by EU or Member State law.

If Tappin believes an instruction infringes the GDPR or other applicable law, it will inform the Controller immediately.

4. Confidentiality

Tappin ensures that persons authorised to process the personal data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality. Access to customer data is strictly limited and logged.

5. Security measures

Tappin implements appropriate technical and organisational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk, including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Role-based access controls with mandatory MFA for staff
  • Audit logs retained for 12 months
  • Annual penetration tests by external providers
  • Documented incident response procedures
  • Regular backups with tested restore procedures
  • Network isolation and least-privilege architecture

6. Subprocessors

The Controller authorises Tappin to engage subprocessors, subject to the conditions in this clause. Tappin maintains a current list of subprocessors and notifies the Controller of any intended changes at least 30 days in advance.

Each subprocessor is bound by a written agreement containing the same data protection obligations as set out in this DPA. The Controller may object to a new subprocessor on reasonable grounds.

Current subprocessors include hosting, email delivery, payment processing, and analytics providers, all located within the EU/EEA.

7. International transfers

Tappin does not transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA unless covered by an appropriate transfer mechanism, typically the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) adopted by the European Commission, supplemented by Transfer Impact Assessments where required.

8. Assistance to the Controller

Tappin assists the Controller, by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as possible, for the fulfilment of the Controller's obligation to respond to requests from data subjects (Articles 12-22) and to comply with Articles 32-36 (security, breach notification, DPIAs, prior consultation).

9. Personal data breach notification

Tappin notifies the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting the Controller's data. The notification will describe:

  • The nature of the breach
  • Categories and approximate number of data subjects affected
  • Likely consequences
  • Measures taken or proposed to address the breach and mitigate its effects

10. Audits

Tappin makes available to the Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this DPA. On reasonable notice, and no more than once per calendar year, the Controller may audit Tappin's processing activities, either directly or through an independent auditor bound by confidentiality.

Tappin shares its annual SOC 2 / ISO 27001 (where applicable) attestations as a first response to audit requests.

11. Deletion & return of data

On termination of the master services agreement, Tappin will, at the Controller's option, return or delete all personal data and copies thereof, unless EU or Member State law requires storage of the personal data. The grace period for data export is 30 days.

12. Liability

The liability of the parties is governed by the master services agreement. Nothing in this DPA limits the rights of data subjects under the GDPR.
