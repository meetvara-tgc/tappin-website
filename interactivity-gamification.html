FAQ

Event engagement platform Norway, common questions

What counts as "engagement" on the Tappin event engagement platform?

Any meaningful interaction beyond passive attendance, voting in a poll, asking or upvoting a Q&A, reacting to a session, sending a meeting request, opening a sponsor profile, or completing a mission. Our 62% benchmark is the share of attendees who actively engage in any of these in a given session.

How do live polls Q&A Norway audiences actually use compare to typical polls?

Multi-choice, scale-based, word clouds, image-driven, and open text. Anonymous mode for sensitive topics. Big-screen reveal with smooth animations so the result becomes part of the show, not a footnote. Moderators can pre-load polls or improvise during the session.

Can we moderate Q&A in real time?

Yes. The moderation queue auto-groups similar questions, lets you approve or merge in one click, and surfaces only approved questions on the speaker-facing view. Anonymous mode is available for sensitive internal events.

How does event gamification Norway work without feeling juvenile?

Missions are designed around real behaviour you want to reward, visit booth A, attend the diversity session, exchange business cards with three speakers. The leaderboard surfaces top participants, sponsor-funded prize tiers reward winners, and anti-gaming logic prevents anyone from spamming polls for points.

Does the engagement layer work the same for hybrid events?

Yes, and this is one of Tappin's signature features. Polls, Q&A and reactions unify in-room and online audiences. A speaker sees one combined result, regardless of where votes came from. Full hybrid feature breakdown on the hybrid event platform Norway page.

What happens to engagement data after the event?

Every interaction is captured, aggregated and exportable. Post-event reports include engagement rate per session, top questions, poll results, and reaction timelines. Data flows to HubSpot, Salesforce or Power BI via native integration.
