Tappin Privacy Policy

The Tappin privacy policy explains how Tappin AS collects, uses, stores and protects personal data on our event platform in Norway, written in plain English, not legalese.

On this page
Introduction What we collect How we use it Legal basis Sharing & subprocessors Retention Your rights Security Cookies Contact us
Last updated: 1 March 2026

Introduction

Tappin AS ("Tappin", "we", "us") is the data controller for personal information collected through tappin.no and the operator of the Tappin event platform. Our office is at Drammensveien 288, 0283 Oslo, Norway. Organization number 999 999 999.

When you visit our website, contact us, or interact with events run on the Tappin platform, we process personal data. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, what we do with it, and the rights you have over your information.

What we collect

We collect data in three contexts:

  • When you visit tappin.no: IP address, browser type, pages visited, referrer, and basic analytics (via cookies, see the Cookies section).
  • When you contact us: name, email, phone, organization, role, country, and the content of your message.
  • When you use a Tappin-powered event as an attendee: the data collected is defined by the event organizer (our customer). Tappin acts as a data processor in that context.

How we use it

We use personal data only for the purposes for which it was collected:

  • To reply to your inquiries and demo requests
  • To deliver and improve the Tappin platform
  • To send you product updates (only if you have opted in)
  • To comply with legal obligations (e.g. accounting, tax, GDPR requests)

We do not sell personal data. We do not profile attendees for advertising purposes. We do not share data with third parties beyond what is necessary to deliver the service.

Legal basis

Our processing of your personal data is based on one or more of the following:

  • Performance of a contract, to deliver the Tappin service to our customers and their attendees
  • Legitimate interest, to respond to inquiries, secure our systems, and improve the product
  • Consent, for marketing communications and non-essential cookies
  • Legal obligation, to meet accounting, tax, and regulatory requirements

Sharing & subprocessors

We share personal data only with carefully vetted subprocessors who help us deliver the service, payment providers, email delivery, hosting infrastructure, and analytics. Each is contractually bound to GDPR-equivalent terms.

A full list of current subprocessors is available on request from post@tappin.no. We notify customers of subprocessor changes through their account.

Personal data is hosted in Norway and processed within the EU/EEA. We do not transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA without appropriate safeguards in place.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as we need it:

  • Website contact form data: 24 months from last interaction
  • Marketing subscriptions: until you unsubscribe
  • Customer event data: for the duration of the customer agreement, plus a 30-day grace period for export
  • Accounting records: 5 years, per Norwegian law

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Lodge a complaint with Datatilsynet (the Norwegian Data Protection Authority)

To exercise any of these rights, email post@tappin.no. We respond within 30 days.

Security

We take security seriously. Data in transit is encrypted via TLS 1.3. Data at rest is encrypted with AES-256. Access to production systems is limited and logged. We perform regular penetration tests and maintain incident response procedures.

In the unlikely event of a personal data breach, we notify affected parties and Datatilsynet within 72 hours, as required by GDPR.

Cookies

We use a minimal set of cookies. Essential cookies (for session management) do not require consent. Analytics cookies (which help us improve the site) are loaded only if you accept them via the cookie banner.

You can change your cookie preferences anytime via the cookie banner or your browser settings.

Tappin privacy policy, quick reference for event organizers

If you're a Norwegian event organizer evaluating Tappin and want the Tappin privacy policy summarised in five lines for an internal review:

  • Data location: Norway and EU/EEA only. No data transferred to the US or other third countries without Standard Contractual Clauses and Transfer Impact Assessments.
  • Data processing role: Tappin acts as the data processor for your attendee data. You remain the data controller. A Data Processing Agreement is signed with every customer.
  • Retention: Customer event data is retained for the term of the contract plus a 30-day export grace period. Accounting records are kept for 5 years per Norwegian law.
  • Subprocessors: A current list is available on request from post@tappin.no. Customers receive 30 days notice of any new subprocessor.
  • Breach notification: Customers are notified within 48 hours of any personal data breach affecting their data. Datatilsynet is notified within 72 hours per Article 33.

For the full compliance position, including how Tappin operates as a GDPR event platform Norway customers can rely on, see the GDPR Information page.
