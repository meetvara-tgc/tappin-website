FAQ

Digital event platform Norway, common questions

How is Tappin different from a webinar tool or virtual event platform Norway?

Webinar tools optimize for broadcast. Generic virtual event platforms try to recreate trade-show booths nobody visits. The Tappin digital event platform Norway is built for participation, branded streaming sits next to real 1:1 networking, gamified missions, live polls, Q&A, and a built-in app that attendees actually open.

Do attendees need to download an app?

No. The Tappin online conference platform runs as a Progressive Web App on any browser, mobile or desktop. Enterprise customers can also offer a native iOS/Android shell. Attendees click one branded link from an email and they're in.

Does the platform handle multilingual digital events?

Yes. Live captions and translations are supported, and the attendee interface can be set per event in Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish, German or Spanish. Useful when your digital audience spans the Nordics.

Can we record sessions and offer on-demand replay?

Yes. Every session can be recorded and pushed to an on-demand library accessible to registered attendees. The library is fully branded with your colors and logo, and analytics show who watched what, including post-event consumption.

What happens to engagement data after a digital event?

Every interaction is captured, session attendance, poll responses, networking activity, Q&A submissions. Data exports to HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft Dynamics and Power BI natively, so digital event leads flow into your existing CRM workflow with zero manual cleanup.

Can we pivot to hybrid or physical mid-cycle?

Yes. The same Tappin platform powers hybrid events and fully in-person physical events. Switch scope without re-platforming. This pivot-ready architecture is one of the main reasons customers chose Tappin during the 2020 disruption, and stayed.
